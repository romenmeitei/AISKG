# Superseded release notice — v3.1.1

AISKG v3.1.1 remains scientifically valid for the corrected benchmark and final pathway endpoint results, but it is superseded by v3.1.2 for public manuscript reproducibility. v3.1.2 adds the completed Expert A, Expert B, and third-expert workbooks, independently recomputes agreement, verifies all adjudications, and reconstructs every final label.
